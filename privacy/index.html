Shelf Control – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Shelf Control ("the app"), made by SonOfACode, helps you limit your own social media use and rewards you for reading. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short

Your data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics. We never collect, receive or sell your data. The only exceptions are the ones you start yourself: looking up a book's ISBN or cover, sharing a book card, buying Shelf Control Plus, and saving a backup where you choose.

What the app uses, and why

Information Why Where it is kept
App usage (which apps are open and for how long), through Android's Usage access permission To measure how much time you spent in the social media apps you chose during the last 24 hours, and to notice when you open one Read on your device only. Not stored or sent anywhere.
Display over other apps permission To show the full-screen READ A BOOK reminder when your limit is reached Not applicable
Book cover photos you take or choose To show your books on your bookshelf Saved inside the app's private storage on your device
Reading entries (book title, author, pages, notes, ratings, reading times) and your settings For the piggy bank (coins), streaks, badges, weekly challenges, vouchers, room designs and stats Saved inside the app's private storage on your device
A book's ISBN number, only when you scan a barcode To look up the book's title and author Sent to Google Books (and, if not found there, to Open Library). Nothing else is sent: no photo, no name, no account.
A book's title and author, only when you tap "Find the cover" for an e-book or audiobook To show the book's cover, so you don't need a photo Sent to Google Books; the chosen cover image is downloaded and saved in the app. Nothing else is sent.
Barcode and cover text recognition To read the barcode and the text on a cover photo Done on your device by Google Play services (ML Kit). The photo is not uploaded. Google may collect limited technical diagnostics about this feature under its own terms.
Reminders (notifications) To remind you of your reading streak, the weekly challenge, coins about to expire and a finished Shelf Control pause. At most one a day; each kind can be turned off in Settings Worked out on your device. No notification service of ours is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.
Your voice, only when you tap the microphone (Shelf Control Plus) To turn what you say into text in your journal or book notes Turned into text on your device by Android's on-device speech recognition. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere; only the text you keep is saved in the app. The microphone permission is asked for the first time you tap it.
Backup file (Shelf Control Plus), only when you choose to back up So you can keep your progress safe and move it to a new phone Saved as one file in the place you pick (for example Google Drive or your phone's Files). It never goes to us. If you pick a cloud service, that service's own privacy policy applies.
Purchase of Shelf Control Plus To unlock Plus Handled entirely by Google Play. We don't receive your payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether Plus is owned.
Book cards you share To show friends a book and the notes you chose Only when you tap Share; the card goes to the app you pick (e.g. a messaging app)

The app does not access your contacts, location, messages, browsing history, or the content of any other app. "Discover the world" opens Google Maps with a search such as "bookstore"; what happens there is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not share any information with anyone. The app only connects to the internet to look up an ISBN you scanned or the cover of an e-book or audiobook you added, and to check your Shelf Control Plus purchase with Google Play. When you share a book card, you decide what is on it and which app it goes to.

Third-party services used on request: - Google Books – https://policies.google.com/privacy - Open Library (Internet Archive) – https://archive.org/about/terms.php - Google ML Kit (on-device) – https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms - Google Play (purchases) – https://policies.google.com/privacy

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or go to Settings → Apps → Shelf Control → Storage → Clear data. This permanently deletes everything the app has stored. The app's data is not included in Android's automatic device backups. A backup file you saved yourself (Plus) stays where you saved it until you delete it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
